# Snug

Daily piece-fitting puzzle with a yarn and patchwork look. Moved here from Val's aurp repo, which now frames `/embed/snug`.

- **Routes**: `/games/snug` (play), `/games/snug/art` (art sheet; every example comes from a pre-launch date, so it spoils nothing), `/embed/snug` (frameless, for iframes; `?share=<url>` sets the share link). All public. `SnugClient.tsx` loads the game browser-only, since it reads `window` and localStorage while rendering. Framing is allowed per site in `next.config.mjs`.

- `../daily.ts` — shared daily helpers: local date key (`?date=YYYY-MM-DD` override, development only), seeded mulberry32 RNG, one-recorded-attempt store (`<game>.attempt.<date>` in localStorage).
- **Month quilts (puzzle version 2, from October 2026)**: every day's puzzle is one patch of its month's quilt. `outline.ts` draws the month's outline (per-month pools in `MONTH_POOLS`, so a year never repeats a shape), cuts it into wavy-edged day regions, and fills each region exactly with the weekday's patch count; `checkMonth` holds every rule. Months are generated ahead with `npm run snug:months -- YYYY-MM [YYYY-MM] [--force]` into `data/snug/months/` (not public) and never change once generated. `app/games/snug/month/[month]` serves only the days already reached (UTC tomorrow; every day in development), and `puzzles.ts` loads them, keeping a copy per month for offline replays. `generator.ts`'s `generatePuzzle` is now only for the art sheet and the lab.
- **Version stamps**: saved runs and unfinished games carry `v` (`version.ts`). Anything without the current version is from the old boards: `clearStaleRecords` removes it from the browser on load, sync ignores it, and the server refuses it. `scripts/migrate-snug-reset.mjs` deletes stale `snug_days` rows (dry run unless `--yes`).
- **Month quilt views** (`monthQuilt.tsx`): `MonthQuiltView` draws each finished day with the player's own patches at its offset (holes as small red crosses); the server sends the outline (`shape`) only once every day is reached, and the client shows it only after the month ends, when missed days appear as dashed linen. The calendar's Quilt tab shows the month so far, or a past month with its score and "Play the reveal". `MonthReveal` plays once per device (`snug.revealed.<YYYY-MM>`) on the first open in a new month when last month has any run; it flies the days from calendar squares into the quilt, then shows the score (on the day, later, missed, perfect days) and shares a 1080 by 1350 picture (share sheet, else a download).
- **Lab**: `/games/snug/lab` (signed in only) sews any outline on demand on a pre-launch sample month, drawn with `MonthQuiltView` as if every day were solved, with shape numbers against `generatePuzzle`.
- `pieces.ts` — 19 polyominoes, `rotateCW` (keeps cell order so a held cell stays under the pointer), `outlinePath`.
- `generator.ts` — reverse tiling: grows a blob from real pieces (board = blob), plus decoys. From `CURVE_START` (2026-10-19) the weekday sets pieces and spares (`levelFor`: Monday Gentle 7+1 up to Sunday Tough 10+3); earlier days keep 8–9 pieces and 2 decoys, generated exactly as before. Rejects holes, fill outside 0.55–0.82, bbox over 9. Chiral shapes get a random handedness at generation; **players can only turn, never flip**, so basket orientations are rotations of the solution.
- `quilt.ts` — yarn palette and the date-seeded patchwork background (7 fabric styles).
- `parts.tsx` — shared visuals (knit patterns, `PatchSvg`, `BoardSvg`, `QuiltBackdrop`, buttons, result card).
- `Snug.tsx` — game: drag with snap, tap / Space / R to turn (works mid-drag), a turned piece that no longer fits hovers loose with a red-thread tint instead of returning to the basket (symmetric turns stay put), timer starts on first placement, auto-finish on zero holes, "Tie it off" for imperfect submits, replays unscored, "Show a perfect fill" after finishing.
- **Past quilts**: arrows or the calendar step back to `LAUNCH_DATE` (backdated to 2026-10-01). A day opens finished on the scored first run, else (past days) on your best practice run. Future days are not reachable except via `?date=`.
- `records.ts` — `snug.best.<date>` keeps the best run from any mode (fewer holes, then no hint, then faster); `dayRecord` gives the calendar status. `calendar.tsx` — month grid opened from the date label (`StitchedModal`), marks perfect / perfect later / scored holes (bold red) / practice holes (faded rust) / in progress.
- **Share**: line, the `share.ts` silhouette (orange filled, brown holes, white outside so every square is the same width; never which patch went where, so it spoils nothing), plus the `shareUrl` prop (default `SHARE_URL`, `https://bstocksharp.dev/games/snug`, even on previews and localhost; the embed's `?share=` overrides it); touch devices with `navigator.share` open the share sheet. Share it as one `text` with the link at the end, never a separate `url`: iOS targets such as Copy and most chat apps then keep only the link. Where the browser can share files, a 1080 square picture of the same silhouette (`drawResultPicture`) goes with the text; it is drawn when the result is recorded, not on the tap, since Safari opens the share sheet only within the tap.
- `stitches.tsx` — hand-sewn running stitches (uneven, wobbly, shadowed, needle holes; no ply dots, by request). `stitchLoops` + `insetCellLoops` for SVG shapes (pieces, binding, basting grid, quilt patches); `StitchBorder` measures its positioned parent for HTML boxes. No CSS dashed outlines left.
- `ArtSheet.tsx` — every visual on one page for art review.
- `collection.tsx` — `QuiltThumb` draws a finished quilt small; the calendar uses it for every played day (gold edge = perfect on the day, gold check = perfect later, red/faded count = scored/practice holes, dashed = in progress), and the art sheet shows a sample month.
- **Home screen**: `/games/snug` has its own app setting (`manifest.webmanifest` route), icon (`app-icon` route: a nine-patch quilt with a sewn "Snug" label at 180px and up) and Apple title, so saving it makes a Snug app. `palette.ts` holds the colors without React, for those server routes.
- **Stats**: `playerStats` in `records.ts` counts scored runs only: played, perfect, streak (days in a row finished on the day, ending today or yesterday), best streak. Shown on the result card.
- **Credit**: `QuiltLabel` ("Sewn by Bryce") hangs off the panel's bottom edge and the result card links "More games by Bryce"; both open bstocksharp.dev/games in a new tab when framed.
- **Fonts**: Caveat and Fredoka come from `next/font` in `fonts.ts`, as CSS variables on Snug's outer element.

- **Header / help**: phones (<560px play width) use a two-row header (title + "?" + stats, then date arrows; past days add a "»" back-to-today arrow). Rules live in the `HowToPlay` modal (auto-opens once, `snug.howToSeen`). Phone result card is a fixed bottom sheet.
- **Phone footer**: stays on one line down to 375px wide: Undo is an icon, buttons use `tight` padding, only one help button shows at a time (the free spare first, then hints), the "not scored" note is left to the result card, and the tie-off question replaces the row while it is asked.
- **Layout**: side-by-side is preferred whenever cells stay >= 30px; otherwise stacked (board on top). Basket slots are packed in rows, sized by each piece's longest side so turning never reflows. Fits width and `innerHeight` minus everything above the play area on the page (`topY`, so a site header counts) and `FOOTER_H`. Touch drags float the piece `1.2` cells above the finger (`Drag.lift`).

- **Controls**: tap / Space / R / E turn clockwise, Q / Shift+R and the turn-back button turn the other way. Undo (button, Ctrl/Cmd+Z) restores whole board snapshots; the hint and finishing clear the history. Keyboard: Tab to a patch, arrows pick it up and move it (`Carry`), Enter stitches it down if it fits, Esc cancels, Backspace sends it to the basket; a carried patch keeps its old spot until Enter, so turning it marks it loose if the turn no longer fits there. Turns pivot on the square last grabbed on that same patch (`anchorFor`), else its first square; moves are announced in an `aria-live` region.
- **Midnight**: `Snug` re-reads today's date every minute and on tab focus. A day keeps scoring by the date it was opened on (`openedOn`), so a game running past midnight stays scored, and a "New quilt" button appears.
- **Accounts**: signed-in players (the page passes the active profile's id) sync with `snug_days` through `sync.ts` and `app/actions/snug.ts`. On load the account is merged in before the day opens (waits up to `SYNC_WAIT_MS`, then plays locally): the account's scored run wins, the better best wins, the longer-played unfinished game wins, and whatever only the browser has is uploaded. After that each finish is saved at once and progress about 2 seconds after it changes (flushed when the page hides). `snug.owner` records whose results the browser holds; when the active person changes, the browser's copy is cleared rather than uploaded to them. Signed-out players and Val's iframe play on the device only. The server keeps the first scored run (`COALESCE`) and replaces the best only with a better one (fewer holes, then hints, then time), in SQL.
- **Saving**: nothing is saved for a day until its clock starts, so opening a day never marks it in progress (older empty saves are ignored by `dayRecord`).
- **Clock**: starts on the first placement and counts active time only; it stops while How to play or the calendar is open (`clock` ref: `base` + running `since`). Every unfinished first run (today's scored game and past-day practice; not replays) saves `snug.progress.<date>` (pieces, elapsed, started, hintId) on every change, tab hide, page hide, and unmount. Today's scored game returns under a "Paused" cover until Resume; practice resumes directly. Finishing clears it.

- **Spare**: at 3 minutes (`SPARE_AT`) one spare can be marked: it turns grey (faded in the basket) but stays usable, and is saved in progress as `pulledIds`. It prefers a spare whose shape the intended fill does not use. It is free: results do not count it.
- **Hints**: after 5 and 10 minutes of active time (`HINT_AT`), each hint places a solution patch (moving anything in its way to the basket). Hinted patches are gold-stitched while they sit in that spot and can still be moved and turned; "Empty the quilt" leaves them while they are there, and a second hint skips a hinted patch the player moved while another is open; `hintIds` is saved in progress so a reload cannot earn more, and runs record `hints` (older runs saved `hinted`, which `hintsUsed` reads as one). Fewer hints rank better, and results and share lines say "with a hint" or "with 2 hints".
- **After finishing**: the result card (with a close X) offers "Show a perfect fill" and "Look at my quilt"; the footer toggles between the two views. "My quilt" is `finalPieces`: the recorded first run when returning, or this session's latest replay (replays are never persisted).

- **Many fills**: almost every day has more than one perfect fill, and most have one that uses a spare (generated October to December 2026: 91 of 92 days have several, 86 have one with a spare). A spare is only left over in the intended fill, so nothing locks patches out, and any fill with no holes finishes as perfect.
- **Seams**: a patch's edge is drawn inside its own squares and stitched-down patches cast no shadow, so neighbors meet at a clean seam instead of seeming to overlap.

## Gotchas
- `QuiltBackdrop` paints the quilt on `body` via GlobalStyles while mounted, so the page's own wrappers must stay transparent.
- Records live in localStorage per site. Inside an iframe the browser keeps a separate store for each embedding site, so aurp players and bstocksharp.dev players have separate records.
- MUI ToggleButton ignores sx selected-state overrides under this theme; the view toggle uses `Btn` instead.
- Changing the generator changes every day's puzzle, which breaks already-saved attempts for those dates.
- Chrome ignores `touch-action` on inner SVG shapes; it must be on the piece's outer `<svg>` or touch drags get a `pointercancel` and scroll the page.
- Phones: the whole play area is `touch-action: none` plus a non-passive `touchmove` preventDefault, and the page has `overscroll-behavior: none`, so missed touches never scroll. Basket slots have invisible hit areas (z 4, under the pieces) that grab the nearest square.
- On touch screens, height-only resizes (address bar showing/hiding) are ignored; re-laying out mid-scroll resized the game and left stale stitch borders on iOS.
- `StitchBorder` renders a new svg (keyed by size) whenever its box resizes; redrawing one in place left stale stitches on iOS Safari when the site header hid or showed.
