Return to Sender

A post office version of The Rule. Six kinds of mail, one for each day from Monday to Saturday, and a Super Sunday that puts one secret regulation on two kinds of mail at once. Each game here has two sample puzzles.

Submission log

    Newest first. Tap an item to bring it back to the counter.

    • Build an item at the counter and press Submit. The clerk stamps it Approved or Return to sender, based on the puzzle's secret regulation.
    • When you think you know the regulation, request an inspection: approve or return 8 new items. All 8 right closes the puzzle.
    • A wrong inspection sends you back to the counter. Your score is how many items you submitted.

    What this mockup leaves out

    • Each game shows two sample puzzles. The real game would draw a new puzzle every day from a much bigger list of regulations.
    • Super Sunday pairs two kinds of mail under one regulation, and the pair changes from week to week. The two samples pair the stamp sheet with the parcel row, and the envelope with the house numbers.
    • The regulations come from short sample lists: 8 for stamp sheets, 10 for parcel rows, and 9 each for envelopes, house numbers, routes and truck loads.
    • Nothing depends on color. Parcels and stamp pictures are told apart by shape, boxes by size and weight, and the four colors are picked to stay distinct for red-green colorblindness.
    • Each inspection approves 3, 4 or 5 of its 8 items, so splitting the bag in half is not a safe guess.
    • No sound, hints, monthly stamp album or real share sheet. The closing memo shows a text preview of the share. The peek button exists only so you can check the mockup quickly.
